From capture to public archive

Captua, Fisqua, and Zasqua connect the work of digitizing collections, describing their contents, and making them available for research. Developed with partner archives, they support institutions and communities in preserving and sharing their own historical materials.

01 · Digitize

Captua

Capture archival materials with a low-cost, offline station built around a Raspberry Pi and paired cameras. Organize volumes, check image quality, and coordinate reviews and retakes to prepare completed captures for cataloging and publication.

02 · Describe and manage

Fisqua

Describe collections, identify people and places, and coordinate archival work. Bring students, volunteers, and community members into collaborative cataloging under professional review.

Explore Fisqua ↗
03 · Publish

Zasqua

Turn cataloged collections into searchable public archives. Make descriptions and high-resolution images available through durable websites that require little infrastructure to maintain.

Digital storytelling

Telar and Telar Compositor support the creation and publication of interpretive work with historical materials—for research, public exhibitions, and teaching.

Publish interactive narratives

Telar

Build stories around digitized objects, bringing images, maps, audio, video, and text into layered visual narratives. Published projects remain portable and inexpensive to maintain.

Author collaboratively

Telar Compositor

Compose Telar stories in a shared visual editor. Assemble materials, frame images, and write interpretive text together, with the work saved in the authors’ own repositories.

Projects

AMPL's projects are research partnerships and collaborations that put the lab's tools and methods to work: digitizing and describing endangered collections, building digital archives and narratives with the communities reflected in them, and developing more egalitarian approaches to preserving and activating the past, in Latin America and beyond.
